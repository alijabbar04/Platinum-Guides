# Platinum Guides

Offline, tick-off trophy guides as installable phone apps. One folder per game.

| Game | Folder | Platform | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red Dead Redemption 2 | [`red-dead-redemption-2/`](red-dead-redemption-2/) | PlayStation (PS4 list, also on PS5) | v1.0.0: Android APK in [Releases](../../releases) |

Each guide is one continuous route from a fresh save to the platinum, with missables placed
before their point of no return, trackers for long-running goals, and progress saved on the
device. Guide content is researched from several community sources, cross-checked, and every
disputed or single-source fact is listed rather than silently picked.

These are unofficial fan projects, not affiliated with any publisher. No game artwork or logos are used.
